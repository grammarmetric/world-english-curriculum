The book

Why Cambridge World English

Your child's classes are built on Cambridge Primary World English (Hodder Education), a course endorsed by Cambridge Assessment International Education for full coverage of its official primary English-as-a-second-language curriculum. It's the same standard used by Cambridge International schools worldwide, which means your child's progress is measured against a recognised international benchmark, not a homemade one.

Stage 5 is written for confident beginners who already know the basics and are ready to build real fluency — describing the past, comparing things, giving advice, and telling stories, all around topics kids actually enjoy: ancient buildings, eco-heroes, baking, and emergency rescues.

At home

How homework works

Every week's class ends with a short homework task assigned through the GrammarMetric web app. It's designed to take 10–15 minutes, not hours — enough to lock in what was covered in class without turning evenings into a fight.

You don't need to teach the material yourself. If your child gets stuck, that's useful information for us — it tells the teacher exactly what to revisit next class.

  • ✓Short practice tasks — a quick quiz on that week's words and grammar, auto-marked so results are instant.
  • ✓A speaking task most weeks — a short recording your child submits through the app.
  • ✓Free audio — official course audio your child can replay at home as often as they like.
  • ✓A weekly self-check — "I can…" statements your child ticks off themselves, building the habit of noticing their own progress.

Good to know

Questions parents ask

What if my child hasn't studied English formally before?

Stage 5 assumes some prior English rather than a completely blank slate — the first class doubles as a friendly check so we can add extra support where it's needed, right from week one.

What if my child is already quite advanced?

Stronger students get extra stretch activities throughout the term, and by the later weeks we start previewing the next level up so they're never marking time.

Do we need to buy anything extra?

No — course materials and the GrammarMetric homework app are included. All you need at home is something to listen on for the audio practice.

What happens after these 12 weeks?

Most students continue into the next stage of the same course. We'll let you know at the Week 12 check-in exactly where your child stands and what we'd recommend next.
