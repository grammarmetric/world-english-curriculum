Why Cambridge World English为什么选择剑桥世界英语

Your child's classes are built on Cambridge Primary World English (Hodder Education), a course endorsed by Cambridge Assessment International Education for full coverage of its official primary English-as-a-second-language curriculum. It's the same standard used by Cambridge International schools worldwide, which means your child's progress is measured against a recognised international benchmark, not a homemade one. 孩子的课程以《剑桥世界英语》（Cambridge Primary World English，Hodder Education 出版）为教材，该教材获剑桥国际考评部（Cambridge Assessment International Education）认证，完整覆盖其官方小学英语作为第二语言课程大纲。这与全球剑桥国际学校采用的标准完全一致，也就是说，孩子的学习进度是按照国际公认的标准来衡量的，而不是我们自定的标准。

Stage 5 is written for confident beginners who already know the basics and are ready to build real fluency — describing the past, comparing things, giving advice, and telling stories, all around topics kids actually enjoy: ancient buildings, eco-heroes, baking, and emergency rescues. 第五级适合已经掌握基础、有一定信心的学习者，帮助孩子建立真正的语言流利度——描述过去发生的事、比较事物、提出建议、讲故事，主题也都是孩子们真正感兴趣的：古代建筑、环保小英雄、烘焙和紧急救援。

How homework works作业是如何进行的

Every week's class ends with a short homework task assigned through the GrammarMetric web app. It's designed to take 10–15 minutes, not hours — enough to lock in what was covered in class without turning evenings into a fight. 每周课后，我们都会通过 GrammarMetric 网页应用布置一项简短的作业。作业设计为10–15分钟即可完成，不会占用太多时间——足以巩固课堂所学，又不会让晚上的作业变成一场"拉锯战"。

You don't need to teach the material yourself. If your child gets stuck, that's useful information for us — it tells the teacher exactly what to revisit next class. 您不需要自己辅导孩子完成作业。如果孩子遇到困难，这些信息对我们也很有价值——它能让老师清楚知道下节课该重点复习哪些内容。

  • ✓ Short practice tasks — a quick quiz on that week's words and grammar, auto-marked so results are instant. 短小练习任务 — 针对当周单词和语法的小测验，系统自动判分，结果即时可见。
  • ✓ A speaking task most weeks — a short recording your child submits through the app. 大多数周都有口语任务 — 孩子通过应用提交一段简短的录音。
  • ✓ Free audio — official course audio your child can replay at home as often as they like. 免费音频 — 官方课程音频，孩子可以在家反复回听。
  • ✓ A weekly self-check — "I can…" statements your child ticks off themselves, building the habit of noticing their own progress. 每周自我检查 — 孩子自己勾选"我能做到……"的清单，养成关注自身进步的习惯。

Questions parents ask家长常见问题

What if my child hasn't studied English formally before?如果孩子之前没有系统学过英语怎么办？

Stage 5 assumes some prior English rather than a completely blank slate — the first class doubles as a friendly check so we can add extra support where it's needed, right from week one.第五级教材假设学生已有一定英语基础，而非完全零基础。第一堂课也会顺便做一次轻松的水平了解，以便从第一周起就针对性地提供额外支持。

What if my child is already quite advanced?如果孩子的英语水平已经比较高怎么办？

Stronger students get extra stretch activities throughout the term, and by the later weeks we start previewing the next level up so they're never marking time.水平较高的孩子在整个学期都会获得额外的拓展练习，并且从学期后段开始提前接触下一级别的内容，确保孩子不会原地踏步。

Do we need to buy anything extra?还需要另外购买其他材料吗？

No — course materials and the GrammarMetric homework app are included. All you need at home is something to listen on for the audio practice.不需要——课程教材和 GrammarMetric 作业应用都已包含在内。在家只需要一台可以播放音频的设备即可。

What happens after these 12 weeks?这12周结束后接下来会怎样？

Most students continue into the next stage of the same course. We'll let you know at the Week 12 check-in exactly where your child stands and what we'd recommend next.大多数学生会继续学习同一教材的下一级别。我们会在第12周的总结中，清楚告知孩子目前的水平以及我们的后续建议。
